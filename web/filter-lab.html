Voice

osc440, the 3rd Wave-style voice from your RP2350 module, unmodified and compiled to WebAssembly: 16 voices, 3 oscillators (wavetable or VA, FM, sync, wave envelopes), two sst-filters models per voice, in series or parallel, 4 envelopes, 4 LFOs, 16-slot matrix. Presets carry the wave envelopes and matrix; the knobs here edit the main parameters. It runs on a sixth Euclidean lane, Am–F–C–G one chord per bar, mixed before Beads and the Vinyl Sim.

A Turing Machine-style loop: each 16th, the bit leaving the register comes back in, flipped with the Change probability. 0% locks the loop, 100% is always new. The lowest 8 bits are the note, quantized to the scale over the range.

A simple sampler: one mono sample in RAM, on its own Euclidean lane. Slice cuts the Start–End region into equal slices, or at its hits (Auto: onset detection, Auto sens sets how many). Each hit plays the slice under its step (the region spread over the bars it lasts) or a random one, cutting off the previous one; a hit inside the slice already playing lets it run. Varispeed changes pitch and length together like tape; Keep time shifts the pitch and keeps the length (40 ms grains). One shot plays the whole region. Resample records the wave lane mix from the next downbeat. Pads audition slices. Load any audio file the browser can decode (or drop one on this page): it is mixed to mono and resampled to 44.1 kHz, up to 47 s here. On the Pi the sample memory is a static array in RAM, sized to taste.

Tides

Ramp Outputs View

Routing

Mutable Instruments Tides 2 as the modulation source: four related outputs (Shift spreads them in phase, amplitude or frequency), each sent to a destination with its own amount. In AD and AR the wave lane's notes trigger it.

Mod matrix

osc440's 16-slot modulation matrix, per voice. Presets fill it; edit any slot here. Slot 16 is Env 3's route, set on the ENV page. Amounts are a share of each destination's range (shown in its own units).

Beads

Quality Seed

Mutable Instruments Beads on the master bus, before the Vinyl Sim. Density at noon makes no grains: turn right for random grains, left for regular ones, or let a seed trigger them.

The SP-303's Vinyl Sim program, decoded from its firmware and run in an emulation of its effects DSP, on the master bus after the filters. Bit-exact with the reference simulator; the Reconstructed compressor is a fitted model, not a confirmed decode. Not yet checked against your SP-505. The SP runs at 44.1 kHz, so the page asks the browser for 44.1 kHz too.

The page keys pick the screen. Display: open-mpk4 firmware gfx.c + boot with Pyoko's screens. Sound: osc440 with sst-filters, the sampler, and Mutable Instruments Marbles, Tides 2 and Beads (MIT, unmodified) in WebAssembly, then the SP-303 Vinyl Sim.
